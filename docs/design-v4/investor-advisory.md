# NORTHLINE / International Investor Advisory — Design reference board V4

**Status:** visual proposal for review; live production pages were not replaced in this phase.  
**Business job:** Turn a broad cross-border property search into a personally relevant brief, with image-led destination discovery.

## Specific live references

**Primary:** [Christie’s International Real Estate Dubai — Portfolio Journal](https://www.christiesrealestatedubai.com/the-journal/category/portfolio/)  
**Supporting:** [Engel & Völkers](https://www.engelvoelkers.com/ae/en)  
**Supporting:** [SERHANT.](https://serhant.com/)  

The references were verified for information architecture and recognizable design patterns. Their live desktop/mobile screenshots, exact font family and pixel measurements have **not** been independently captured or authenticated in this review; do not treat proposed tokens as copied source styles.

## Patterns deliberately adapted
- Warm editorial photography and curated destinations instead of financial dashboard imagery
- Very large serif headlines used as story rather than conversion noise
- Destination index to lead into a buyer qualification flow

## Do not copy
- Editorial images and branded property collections
- Financial claims and actual current listing/investment returns
- Exact layout or proprietary content

## Prior defect → New composition

**Prior:** Financial landing visual conventions and insufficient differentiation of global markets; stock-style image repeats.

**Proposed:** An editorial two-column publication layout with Mediterranean imagery, quiet destination chapters and a different inner-page pattern: an image-supported seven-step investor brief. Yield analysis remains available below the core story in the working site.

**Distinct design system:** Paper #F5F2E9; ink #1E292B; dark ocean #102D3A; copper #A57650; Georgia display + restrained sans.

**How this differs from other four websites:** Bright journal editorial, warm type and asymmetrical side-by-side image, not a dark listing search or heavy financial dashboard.
