# SERA / COAST — Developer Microsite — Design reference board V4

**Status:** visual proposal for review; live production pages were not replaced in this phase.  
**Business job:** Tell one coherent architectural story, where atmosphere supports the project’s residences, masterplan and registration.

## Specific live references

**Primary:** [ORLA, Dorchester Collection by OMNIYAT](https://orla.omniyat.com/)  
**Supporting:** [OMNIYAT](https://omniyat.com/)  
**Supporting:** [Ellington — The Meriva Collection](https://the-meriva-collection.ellingtonproperties.ae/)  

The references were verified for information architecture and recognizable design patterns. Their live desktop/mobile screenshots, exact font family and pixel measurements have **not** been independently captured or authenticated in this review; do not treat proposed tokens as copied source styles.

## Patterns deliberately adapted
- Monumental architecture and deliberate negative space
- An expressive material-specific masterplan/residence detail story
- Project-specific images and film with the same architectural identity

## Do not copy
- ORLA video or rendered photos
- Named brand partners and architects
- Exact typography, animation sequences and sales claims

## Prior defect → New composition

**Prior:** Images depicted inconsistent buildings and geographies. Video montage was unrelated to one coherent development.

**Proposed:** Architecture-magazine first screen showing a single fictional limestone coastal residence, plus a dark editorial masterplan interior view that describes arrival, garden living, water terrace and private rooms. Future media will retain one consistent building identity.

**Distinct design system:** Bone #EAE5DC; charcoal #24201D; terracotta #8D5E50; elegant Georgia serif; generous spatial compositions.

**How this differs from other four websites:** The only architecture-only full-bleed filmic site, with no faux marketplace search or transactional filter surface.
