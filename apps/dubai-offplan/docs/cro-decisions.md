# CRO decisions

**Business problem.** Answer factual purchase questions before asking for enquiry. Capture qualifying info without suggesting investment guarantees.

**Primary conversion.** qualified project enquiry with budget/type/purpose/timeline/financing + email/phone.

**Design hypotheses.** Query match in hero, direct jump nav, PDF as optional CTA, lead form after facts, mobile bar for access, no fake stock/discount claims.

**Trust constraints.** Do not fabricate past successes, counts of buyers, transaction rates, reviews, developer credentials, returns or agency licenses. Demo forms must announce whether sending is active. No fake scarcity banners, countdowns or popups.

**Measurement gate.** Use live qualified conversion counts only after connecting a permitted CRM endpoint and gaining consent. No fictitious conversion uplift.
